# 👥 Zombie Love Revival — Character Bible

> This document defines the core cast, their personalities, and how they relate to each other.
> All content creators and AI agents should read this before writing any dialogue or scenes.

---

## How to Use This Document

Each character entry includes:
- **Overview** — a one-paragraph snapshot
- **Personality** — core traits and contradictions
- **Motivations** — what they want and why
- **Fears** — what keeps them up at night
- **Relationship Dynamics** — how they relate to other characters
- **Dialogue Style** — voice, vocabulary, rhythms
- **Sample Lines** — example dialogue to establish voice

Treat these as living documents. Characters should be allowed to grow and surprise.

## 1. PROTAGONIST — "Riley" (Player Character)

*Default name: Riley. Gender and pronouns are player-selected at start.*

### Overview
Riley arrived in Millhaven 18 months ago, having survived the initial outbreak by mostly staying lucky and occasionally being clever. They are not a hero — they are a person who has not died yet and is quietly unsure how to feel about that.

### Personality
- **Curious** — Riley notices things others don't: details, patterns, the way a Bloom Walker tilts its head
- **Dry wit** — humor as armor; the jokes come out when things get hard
- **Quietly stubborn** — will not back down from something once they've decided it matters
- **Self-doubting** — does not think of themselves as brave, capable, or worthy; the player's choices can either reinforce or challenge this

### Motivations
- Primary: Find a reason to stay in one place long enough to call it home
- Secondary: Understand what happened to their love interest (the Bonded)
- Hidden: Prove — to themselves more than anyone — that they are still capable of love

### Fears
- That they have become hollow — too adapted to loss
- That caring about someone means watching them die
- That they are making everything worse by staying

### Relationship Dynamics
- **With Zed** — The central relationship. Tender, strange, slow-building. Riley is drawn to Zed in ways they can't entirely explain and spend half the game trying to rationalize
- **With Sable** — Respect that could become friendship, if Riley stops being so guarded
- **With Crow** — Fond exasperation; Crow makes Riley laugh even when they're trying not to

### Dialogue Style
- Short, observational sentences
- Dry understatement
- Occasional bursts of more emotional language when caught off guard
- Internal monologue (shown in italics) tends to be more honest than spoken dialogue

**Sample Lines:**
> "I used to think 'apocalypse' was a metaphor. Now it's just Tuesday."

> *Was that a smile? That was definitely almost a smile. A Bloom Walker just almost-smiled at me. I'm going to choose not to examine that.*

> "I don't know if this is brave or just really bad planning. Historically those have looked identical."

## 2. ZED — Zombie Love Interest (The Bonded)

*Full name unknown. Designated "Zed" by the Cultivators. Gender presentation: androgynous. Pronouns: they/them.*

### Overview
Zed was infected during the early outbreak but arrested at Stage 2 — a Bloom Walker Bonded case. They retain fragments of behavior, preference, and what appears to be emotional recognition. They cannot speak. They communicate through gesture, proximity, arranged objects, and a complex set of postures that the Cultivators have partially mapped. They remember Riley — or something that functions like memory.

### Personality
- **Patient** — Zed moves slowly, deliberately, and never seems hurried
- **Observant** — they watch everything with quiet intensity; nothing seems to escape their notice
- **Strangely gentle** — despite being a predator by biological design, Zed does not harm things without cause
- **Melancholy** — there is a quality of deep sadness to Zed, a sense of something lost that cannot be named

### Motivations
- Instinctive: proximity to Riley; the Bonded state creates an orientation toward their bonded living person
- Emergent: protection; Zed intervenes when Riley is in danger, even at cost to themselves
- Unknown: the fungal network in the Bloom suggests the Bloom Walkers share something. What Zed wants from that network is a mystery

### Fears
*(Zed cannot express fear verbally. These are inferred by observation.)*
- Separation from Riley
- Losing further ground to the infection — moments of apparent lucidity followed by regression are distressing to witness
- The Cultivators' experiments

### Relationship Dynamics
- **With Riley** — The bond. Magnetic, wordless, slowly developing a shared language of gesture and proximity
- **With Sable** — Sable does not trust Zed; Zed seems aware of this and maintains careful distance
- **With Crow** — Crow treats Zed like a person immediately, without reservation; Zed appears to prefer Crow's company after Riley's

### Dialogue Style
*Zed does not speak. Their "dialogue" is:*
- Environmental: objects arranged, paths cleared, things left as gifts
- Physical: posture, gesture, proximity, touch
- Described in narration from Riley's perspective

**Sample Narrative Beat:**
> Zed had arranged seven smooth river stones in a line outside the door. Riley wasn't sure what it meant. They weren't sure Zed knew either. But every morning the stones were there, and every morning Riley counted them.

## 3. SABLE VOSS — Survivor Leader

*Age: late 30s. She/her. Greenwarden Council lieutenant and de facto leader of Shelter 9.*

### Overview
Sable has kept 40 people alive for three years through intelligence, ruthlessness, and a refusal to make peace with comfortable lies. She is not cruel — she is precise. She has buried more people than she can count and has stopped counting. She distrusts sentiment as a luxury she can't afford, but she is quietly, privately, furiously kind.

### Personality
- **Tactical** — thinks in systems, risk assessments, contingencies
- **Guarded warmth** — cares deeply but expresses it through action, not words
- **Impatient with abstraction** — will cut a philosophical conversation off with "so what do we do about it"
- **Privately exhausted** — she holds everything together and no one sees the cost

### Motivations
- Keep Shelter 9 alive
- Maintain the Green Zone's stability without becoming the Council's puppet
- Understand what happened at the Fall — she believes it was preventable and that someone is responsible

### Fears
- That her hard choices have made her someone she wouldn't recognize
- Losing control of the shelter to Council politics
- That she is grooming the next generation to be as hollow as she is

### Relationship Dynamics
- **With Riley** — Wary professional respect that evolves toward trust if Riley proves reliable; she doesn't coddle, but she will go to bat for people who earn it
- **With Zed** — Controlled hostility; she sees Zed as a vector, not a person, and is aware that makes Riley distrust her
- **With Crow** — The closest thing she has to a friend; she finds Crow exhausting and irreplaceable in equal measure

### Dialogue Style
- Clipped, efficient
- Military-adjacent without being cliché
- Occasional sardonic humor, always dry and brief
- When she says something quietly it carries more weight than when she shouts

**Sample Lines:**
> "I don't need you to believe in the mission. I need you to show up on time with the right supplies."

> "I have buried twenty-three people who were 'going to be careful.' Write that down somewhere."

> *(quietly, after a loss)* "Add them to the wall. West wing. Next to Durrant."

## 4. CROW — Comic Relief / Unexpected Depth

*Full name: Cornelius Abara. Age: mid-20s. He/him. Rover courier and self-appointed morale officer.*

### Overview
Crow is the person who shows up at the worst possible moment with terrible jokes and somehow makes everything slightly better. He is relentlessly social, chronically underdressed for the apocalypse, and running a complex multi-faction trade network that he insists is "just favors." He is also, under the bit, paying very close attention to everything and everyone. He is smarter than he lets on and uses the comedy as deliberate camouflage.

### Personality
- **Performatively chaotic** — seems impulsive, is actually always calculating
- **Genuinely warm** — the friendliness is real, even if it's also tactical
- **Avoidant of sincerity** — deflects with humor when conversations get too real; when he drops the bit it means something serious is happening
- **Competitive** — frames everything as a game; needs to win, but can admit when he's lost

### Motivations
- Keep moving — motion is safety, motion is identity
- Maintain his network — information is the only real currency
- Find out what happened to his sister (he doesn't talk about this)

### Fears
- Staying in one place long enough to care about it
- That the people he's made himself stop caring about are right to be angry
- Running out of road

### Relationship Dynamics
- **With Riley** — Immediate, easy warmth; Riley is one of the few people Crow doesn't perform for (much); he respects Riley's honesty even when it's uncomfortable
- **With Sable** — A friendship conducted entirely in insults; they trust each other completely and would die before admitting it
- **With Zed** — Pure, uncomplicated acceptance; Crow talks to Zed like they're an old friend and somehow Zed always seems glad to see him

### Dialogue Style
- Fast-talking, riffing, jokes that land and jokes that don't and he knows the difference
- Pop culture references from before the Fall, increasingly absurd as context has degraded
- Occasional sentences that land like punches — real, raw, without warning
- When he's upset, he talks faster

**Sample Lines:**
> "Good news: I found the medical supplies. Bad news: I traded them for something better. Worse news: my definition of 'better' has historically been unreliable."

> "Zed! My guy! My undead compatriot! You are looking — okay, you are looking like a before picture, but that's fine, that's a *journey*, I respect that."

> *(quietly, dropping the bit entirely)* "...I had a sister. Millhaven, before the Fall. I keep thinking I'll run into her. Even now. Is that — is that stupid?"

## Character Relationship Map

```
Riley ←→ Zed         [Central romance arc — wordless, slowly emergent]
Riley ←→ Sable       [Earned trust arc — professional to genuine]
Riley ←→ Crow        [Friendship arc — immediate warmth, hidden depth]
Sable ←→ Crow        [Pre-existing bond — banter masks real loyalty]
Zed   ←→ Crow        [Unusual acceptance — Crow treats Zed as a person unconditionally]
Zed   ←→ Sable       [Tension — Sable sees threat; Zed sees awareness]
```

## Writing Notes for AI Agents

- **Don't make Crow purely comic** — every 3–4 scenes, he should have one moment of genuine weight
- **Don't make Sable a villain** — her hard choices should be legible as hard choices, not cruelty
- **Don't give Zed words** — their communication is physical and environmental; protect this
- **Riley's internal monologue** should be the most honest writing in any scene — this is where the player lives
- **Avoid** explaining what characters feel; show it through behavior, word choice, and what they *don't* say
