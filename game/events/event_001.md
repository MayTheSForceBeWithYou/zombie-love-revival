# Event 001 — The Turn — The Monster You Choose to Become

*Seed: Scenario 5 — The Monster You Choose to Become.*

## Event Summary

The player is bitten in the opening hour, dies, and wakes up still themselves — and changing.

## Trigger Condition

Start of Act I, the hospital origin scene.

## Characters Involved

- Player
- Crow (on the radio)
- The Static crew

## Player Choice

- Hide the bite and pass as human
- Reveal it and face the crew's fear
- Lash out as the change first hits

## Consequences

- Establishes the transformation system
- Sets initial trust with The Static
- Crow catches wind and starts the legend

## Sample Scene Beats

- A bite, a blackout, and then — waking up thinking
- Veins beginning to glow faintly under the skin
- The crew levels weapons, unsure what they're looking at
- Crow's radio crackles: 'Got a live one who's supposed to be dead. Story's starting.'
