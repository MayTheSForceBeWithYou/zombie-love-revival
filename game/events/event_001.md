# Event 001 — The First Stillness — The Cure Was Love

*Seed: Scenario 1 — The Cure Was Love.*

## Event Summary

The player witnesses someone go Stilled in real time and discovers, by accident, that they can step inside the grief.

## Trigger Condition

Start of Act I, first visit to the mall hub.

## Characters Involved

- Player
- Crow (narrating)
- A newly Stilled stranger

## Player Choice

- Step into the grief and witness it
- Refuse and walk away (delays the gift, hardens Crow's narration)

## Consequences

- Unlocks the witnessing mechanic
- First memory cost is previewed but not charged
- Crow names the stranger, establishing the elegy motif

## Sample Scene Beats

- A stranger freezes mid-reach for a photo
- The player's bare hand frosts; the world goes quiet
- Inside: one looping unbearable moment
- Crow, softly: 'Say you saw it. That's all they need.'
