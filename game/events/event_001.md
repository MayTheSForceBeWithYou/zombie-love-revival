# Event 001 — Welcome to the Committee — The Last Town on Earth

*Seed: Scenario 2 — The Last Town on Earth.*

## Event Summary

Within hours of arriving, the player is processed, badged, and 'volunteered' onto the Coexistence Committee.

## Trigger Condition

Start of Act I, intake at the mall.

## Characters Involved

- Player
- Sable
- Crow

## Player Choice

- Accept the seat graciously
- Try to refuse (Sable assigns it anyway, funnier)
- Negotiate for something in return

## Consequences

- Establishes the political-relationship system
- Sets initial standing with the Living Caucus
- Crow attaches himself as the player's guide

## Sample Scene Beats

- A clerk slaps a sticker over the player's single-letter name
- Sable: 'Congratulations, you're a swing vote now.'
- Crow whispers the real power map behind the bureaucracy
- The player's first folding chair awaits
