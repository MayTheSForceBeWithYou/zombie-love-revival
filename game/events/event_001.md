# Event 001 — The Count — Love at the End of the World

*Seed: Scenario 4 — Love at the End of the World.*

## Event Summary

The player learns the town has a known, finite time left, and is invited to decide how to spend it.

## Trigger Condition

Start of Act I, arrival at the mall countdown wall.

## Characters Involved

- Player
- Sable
- Crow

## Player Choice

- Commit to a 'last good thing' project
- Plan to spend the end alone
- Refuse to accept the count is real

## Consequences

- Establishes the countdown structure
- Sets the player's starting outlook (Maker/Goer/Holdout)
- Crow begins his 'book of last good days'

## Sample Scene Beats

- Crow chalks a new, smaller number on the wall
- Sable: 'We can't save it. We can still choose how it goes.'
- The player sees the town deciding how to spend the season
- An invitation: 'So. What do you want the end to look like?'
