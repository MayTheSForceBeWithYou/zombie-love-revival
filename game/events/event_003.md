# Event 003 — The Becoming — The Monster You Choose to Become

*Seed: Scenario 5 — The Monster You Choose to Become.*

## Event Summary

The transformation reaches its threshold; the player chooses the shape of what they are about to permanently become.

## Trigger Condition

Gate to Act III, the squat or ritual hall.

## Characters Involved

- Player
- Sable
- Riley
- Crow

## Player Choice

- Become a protector of both living and dead (savior)
- Become the end of the old world (destroyer)
- Become something with no name and walk your own road

## Consequences

- Selects the ending branch
- Resolves Sable's and Riley's loyalties
- Reveals Crow's hand in steering the legend

## Sample Scene Beats

- The body finishes changing into something new
- Sable lowers — or raises — her weapon for the last time
- Riley asks if they were loved or worshipped; the player answers
- Crow, off-air at last: 'Your myth. Your ending. Write it.'
