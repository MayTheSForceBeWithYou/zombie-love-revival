# Event 003 — Your Own Door — The Undead Remember

*Seed: Scenario 3 — The Undead Remember.*

## Event Summary

The trail of recovered memories leads to the player's own forgotten past and their role on the night of the Fall.

## Trigger Condition

Gate to Act III, the safehouse/cemetery.

## Characters Involved

- Player
- Crow
- Sable or Riley (route-dependent)

## Player Choice

- Recover your own memory and learn the truth
- Refuse and choose who you'll be now
- Let Crow decide whether to return it

## Consequences

- Determines the ending branch
- Reframes every prior recovered memory
- Crow finally offers the memory he's been holding

## Sample Scene Beats

- The wall of names has one blank space left — the player's
- Crow holds out a single object: 'This is yours.'
- The player stands at their own door
- Whatever's behind it, they choose what to do with it
