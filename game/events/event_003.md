# Event 003 — The Devoted's Plea — The Cure Was Love

*Seed: Scenario 1 — The Cure Was Love.*

## Event Summary

The Devoted beg the player to stop reviving and instead preserve the Stilled as they are, framing healing as a kind of violence.

## Trigger Condition

Cemetery, gate to Act III.

## Characters Involved

- Player
- The Devoted keeper
- Crow

## Player Choice

- Agree to preserve rather than heal
- Refuse and continue reviving
- Seek a third way that costs a relationship

## Consequences

- Locks or unlocks specific endings
- Shifts how much 'self' the player must spend in the finale
- Crow returns a fragment of the player's name as a reward for the hardest choice

## Sample Scene Beats

- The cemetery glows with candle-lit Stilled
- The keeper: 'You call it saving. We call it ending them twice.'
- The player must define what healing means
- Crow: 'Whatever you choose, I'll remember it for you.'
