# Event 003 — The Last Light — Love at the End of the World

*Seed: Scenario 4 — Love at the End of the World.*

## Event Summary

As the count runs out, the player decides how they and the people they love will meet the end.

## Trigger Condition

Gate to Act III, the clifftop safehouse.

## Characters Involved

- Player
- Sable
- Riley
- Crow

## Player Choice

- Meet the end together, here, with your chosen person
- Send loved ones off and stay to close the town down
- Leave together to spend the last days somewhere new

## Consequences

- Selects the final ending
- Defines each character's last moments
- Determines who keeps Crow's book

## Sample Scene Beats

- The countdown wall reads a single digit
- The town gathers, or scatters, by the player's choices
- A last sunset; the decision is made without spectacle
- Crow closes the book: 'I'll keep the light on till the end of the page.'
