# Event 002 — The Sign — The Monster You Choose to Become

*Seed: Scenario 5 — The Monster You Choose to Become.*

## Event Summary

The player's growing pull draws the dead and births a cult, while the Cordon marks the player for elimination.

## Trigger Condition

## Characters Involved

- Player
- Riley (Choir)
- Sable (Cordon)
- Crow

## Player Choice

- Reject it and insist you're just a person
- Use the following for your own ends
