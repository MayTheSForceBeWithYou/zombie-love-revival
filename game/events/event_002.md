# Event 002 — The Casserole Incident — The Last Town on Earth

*Seed: Scenario 2 — The Last Town on Earth.*

## Event Summary

A cross-faction potluck becomes a diplomatic crisis when a Lucid's dish is rejected; the player must defuse or escalate.

## Trigger Condition

Mid Act II, first Lucid League potluck (cemetery).

## Characters Involved

- Player
- Riley
- Living Caucus members
- Crow

## Player Choice

- Publicly eat Riley's dish and back the Lucids
- Smooth it over without taking sides
- Side with the Living Caucus

## Consequences

- Swings faction standing toward the Vote
- Advances or stalls the Riley romance
- Crow turns the incident into town legend on his radio show

## Sample Scene Beats

- A Caucus member refuses to eat 'dead people's cooking'
- Riley, deflating, starts to pack up the soup
- The player's choice freezes the whole gym
- Crow narrates the fallout like a sports highlight
