# Event 002 — One Good Day — Love at the End of the World

*Seed: Scenario 4 — Love at the End of the World.*

## Event Summary

The town pours its energy into a single perfect day — a festival, a play, a wedding — knowing it may be among the last.

## Trigger Condition

Mid Act II, the school or mall.

## Characters Involved

- Player
- Sable
- Riley
- Crow
- Townsfolk

## Player Choice

- Spend the day on the romance you're building
- Spend it serving the whole town (legacy path)
- Spend it finishing private unfinished business

## Consequences

- Strongly weights which ending the player heads toward
- Deepens the chosen romance or the legacy route
- Crow records the day in his book

## Sample Scene Beats

- The town strings lights and pretends, beautifully
- A perfect hour the player gets to fully choose how to spend
- Someone says the quiet part: 'This might be the one we remember.'
- Crow: 'Day's going in the book. Make it count.'
