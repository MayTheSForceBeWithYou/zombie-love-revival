# Cemetery — The Last Town on Earth

*Seed: Scenario 2 — The Last Town on Earth.*

## Description

Greenhaven's cemetery is, ironically, where the Lucids host the town's best parties — the living are warming up to the invitation.

## Mood

Warm, irreverent, secretly the most welcoming place in town.

## Gameplay Purpose

Lucid League social hub; cross-faction bonding and Riley's route beats.

## Important NPCs / Encounters

- Riley hosting a Lucid potluck
- A Lucid elder with great gossip

## Possible Events

- A Lucid potluck the player is nervously invited to
- Riley asks the player to a 'date' among the headstones

## Visual / Audio Notes

String lights in trees, a wheezy accordion, surprisingly good snacks.
