# Cemetery — The Undead Remember

*Seed: Scenario 3 — The Undead Remember.*

## Description

Less a graveyard than a memorial garden — a growing wall of recovered names where the town honors who its dead were.

## Mood

Peaceful, reverent, hopeful rather than grim.

## Gameplay Purpose

Reflection hub; the wall of names visibly tracks the player's recovery progress.

## Important NPCs / Encounters

- Crow tending the wall and reading names aloud
- A survivor searching the wall for someone they lost

## Possible Events

- Adding a fully recovered person's name to the wall
- Crow shares a name he's been holding for the player

## Visual / Audio Notes

Wind chimes, birdsong returning to the town, soft scratch of names being carved.
