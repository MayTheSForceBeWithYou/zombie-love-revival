# Cemetery — Love at the End of the World

*Seed: Scenario 4 — Love at the End of the World.*

## Description

A cemetery where the gentle dead drift among the graves of the recently passed — the living and dead grieving side by side.

## Mood

Luminous melancholy; peace at the edge of the sea.

## Gameplay Purpose

Reflection hub; Riley's route and meditations on letting go.

## Important NPCs / Encounters

- Riley, drifting and lucid in turns
- Crow, marking each new grave

## Possible Events

- A quiet date with Riley among the stones
- Holding a funeral that doubles as a celebration

## Visual / Audio Notes

Surf on rocks, gulls, wind through tall grass, a single bell buoy.
