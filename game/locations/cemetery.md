# Cemetery — The Cure Was Love

*Seed: Scenario 1 — The Cure Was Love.*

## Description

An overgrown cemetery where the Devoted tend the Stilled among the graves, refusing to heal them — only to keep them.

## Mood

Romantic decay; ivy, marble, and devotion gone strange.

## Gameplay Purpose

Faction hub for the Devoted; site of the central moral debate about healing vs. preserving.

## Important NPCs / Encounters

- The Devoted's gentle, unsettling keeper
- Crow, who comes here to name the dead

## Possible Events

- The Devoted ask the player to stop reviving
- Crow returns a fragment of the player's lost name

## Visual / Audio Notes

Rain on stone, distant bells, candle-glow between headstones.
