# Cemetery — The Monster You Choose to Become

*Seed: Scenario 5 — The Monster You Choose to Become.*

## Description

An underpass-and-graveyard sprawl where the dead gather around the player's presence, and shrines to the New Ones bloom in the concrete.

## Mood

Eerie, mythic, strangely tender.

## Gameplay Purpose

Site of the player's growing pull on the dead; key transformation and 'something new' beats.

## Important NPCs / Encounters

- The gathered dead, drawn to the player
- A Cordon sniper deciding whether to take the shot

## Possible Events

- The dead kneel — or rally — around the player
- Sable finds the player here and lowers her weapon

## Visual / Audio Notes

Low collective moan turned almost musical, wind in the underpass, distant radio static.
