# Safehouse — The Undead Remember

*Seed: Scenario 3 — The Undead Remember.*

## Description

The community safehouse, rebuilt room by room as more people are recovered — a literal measure of the town's healing.

## Mood

Warm, growing, full of returning life.

## Gameplay Purpose

Home base; relationship scenes and reviewing recovered memories.

## Important NPCs / Encounters

- Sable coordinating the rebuild
- Riley settling into their recovered self

## Possible Events

- A shared evening reviewing a recovered memory together
- The safehouse gains a room as someone is fully restored

## Visual / Audio Notes

Hammering in the next room, kettle and conversation, a record player relearning its needle.
