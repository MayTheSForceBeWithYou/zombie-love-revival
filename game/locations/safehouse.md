# Safehouse — The Cure Was Love

*Seed: Scenario 1 — The Cure Was Love.*

## Description

Sable's lantern-lit refuge for the few remaining living, walls papered with the names of everyone lost.

## Mood

Warm, fragile, fiercely guarded against the cold outside.

## Gameplay Purpose

Home base; relationship scenes, rest, and the wall of names that tracks the player's choices.

## Important NPCs / Encounters

- Sable, keeping vigil
- Riley, when not at the school

## Possible Events

- A quiet evening that deepens a romance
- The wall gains — or loses — a name based on the player's revivals

## Visual / Audio Notes

Lantern hum, rain on the roof, the scratch of a pen adding a name.
