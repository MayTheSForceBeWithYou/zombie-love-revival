# Safehouse — Love at the End of the World

*Seed: Scenario 4 — Love at the End of the World.*

## Description

A clifftop house with the best view of the last sunsets — the place everyone gathers to spend the evenings that remain.

## Mood

Warm, candle-lit, precious.

## Gameplay Purpose

Home base; the romance and 'how to spend the end' choices culminate here.

## Important NPCs / Encounters

- Sable, planning one more good day
- Crow, keeping the book of last good days

## Possible Events

- A shared sunset that defines a romance
- Deciding together how to spend the final days

## Visual / Audio Notes

Fire crackle, ocean wind, a record played one more time, quiet laughter.
