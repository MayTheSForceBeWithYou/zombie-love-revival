# Safehouse — The Monster You Choose to Become

*Seed: Scenario 5 — The Monster You Choose to Become.*

## Description

A fortified squat that serves as The Static's home base — tag-covered, fairy-lit, bristling with salvage and bad attitude.

## Mood

Defiant warmth; a chosen family in the dark.

## Gameplay Purpose

Home base; relationship scenes and the choices that shape the player's becoming.

## Important NPCs / Encounters

- Sable, off-duty and conflicted
- Crow, broadcasting from the roof

## Possible Events

- A rooftop talk that defines a romance
- The crew confronts the player about what they're becoming

## Visual / Audio Notes

Pirate radio low, spray-paint and solder smell, generator thrum, laughter over the static.
