# Safehouse — The Last Town on Earth

*Seed: Scenario 2 — The Last Town on Earth.*

## Description

The player's assigned bunk in a converted storefront — small, drafty, and immediately colonized by drop-in visitors.

## Mood

Cozy chaos; everyone treats it like a clubhouse.

## Gameplay Purpose

Home base; rest, relationship check-ins, and plotting before the Vote.

## Important NPCs / Encounters

- Crow letting himself in uninvited
- Whichever love interest is dropping by 'to talk strategy'

## Possible Events

- A late-night strategy session that turns personal
- The player decides which faction to back before the Vote

## Visual / Audio Notes

Space heater tick, kettle whistle, Crow's radio playing low.
