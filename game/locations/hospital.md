# Hospital — The Last Town on Earth

*Seed: Scenario 2 — The Last Town on Earth.*

## Description

The clinic doubles as the town's most contested building — who gets the last antibiotics is the question that decides elections.

## Mood

Tense, antiseptic, faintly comic in its scarcity.

## Gameplay Purpose

Resource-politics site; choices here move faction standing toward the Vote.

## Important NPCs / Encounters

- An overworked medic refereeing living-vs-Lucid care
- A Lucid patient who just wants to be triaged fairly

## Possible Events

- Allocating scarce meds and angering a faction
- Discovering the rationing irregularity Crow hinted at

## Visual / Audio Notes

Squeaky gurney wheels, a vending machine that only has gum, muffled arguing through walls.
