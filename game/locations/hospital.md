# Hospital — The Cure Was Love

*Seed: Scenario 1 — The Cure Was Love.*

## Description

A flooded hospital where the heaviest griefs pooled; the Stilled here are dense with sorrow and dangerous to witness.

## Mood

Drowned, reverent, heavy as held breath.

## Gameplay Purpose

Mid-game site for high-cost revivals; introduces the memory-erosion stakes.

## Important NPCs / Encounters

- A Stilled doctor frozen over an empty gurney
- Sable's hidden Stilled, if her route is active

## Possible Events

- A revival that costs the player a named memory
- Discovering the ledger of who Sable could not save

## Visual / Audio Notes

Ankle-deep water, distant monitor tones with no patients, flickering emergency light.
