# Hospital — The Monster You Choose to Become

*Seed: Scenario 5 — The Monster You Choose to Become.*

## Description

A gutted hospital where the player first wakes up turned, now a place where the Choir studies the 'New Ones' and the Cordon studies how to stop them.

## Mood

Charged, sacred-and-clinical, faintly menacing.

## Gameplay Purpose

Origin site; transformation milestones and the savior/destroyer stakes are revealed here.

## Important NPCs / Encounters

- A Choir 'physician' documenting the player's changes
- A captured Cordon medic afraid of what they're seeing

## Possible Events

- A transformation milestone the player must interpret
- Choosing whether to spare a Cordon prisoner

## Visual / Audio Notes

Buzzing failing lights, dripping, the hum the dead make near the player.
