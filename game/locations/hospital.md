# Hospital — Love at the End of the World

*Seed: Scenario 4 — Love at the End of the World.*

## Description

The hospital is now hospice for the whole town — a place to make endings gentle rather than to cure anything.

## Mood

Tender, unhurried, achingly kind.

## Gameplay Purpose

Site for the game's most direct meditations on mortality and the 'how to end' choices.

## Important NPCs / Encounters

- A hospice volunteer who's made peace with it all
- Sable, if her hidden illness is revealed here

## Possible Events

- Sitting with someone through their last good day
- Learning the truth about Sable's health

## Visual / Audio Notes

Soft monitors turned to comfort settings, open windows, sea air, low conversation.
