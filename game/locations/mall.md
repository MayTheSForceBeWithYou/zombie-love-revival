# Mall — The Monster You Choose to Become

*Seed: Scenario 5 — The Monster You Choose to Become.*

## Description

A blacked-out mall reborn as a scavenger-punk market and venue, every surface tagged with glyphs and gig flyers, generators throbbing in the dark.

## Mood

Loud, defiant, electric. Alive in the ruins.

## Gameplay Purpose

Hub for The Static crew; gear, gigs, and the player's first taste of being treated as a sign.

## Important NPCs / Encounters

- A Static crew boss who runs the market
- A graffiti-shaman tagging the player 'for luck'

## Possible Events

- A pirate-radio gig where Crow broadcasts the player's legend
- A Cordon raid forces the player to use their new abilities in public

## Visual / Audio Notes

Generator hum, distorted guitar, spray-can hiss, neon and blacklight.
