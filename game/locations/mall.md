# Mall — Love at the End of the World

*Seed: Scenario 4 — Love at the End of the World.*

## Description

A half-lit mall the town uses for its last communal projects — a greenhouse in the atrium, a stage in the food court, a wall counting down the days.

## Mood

Golden-hour wistful; beauty made more vivid by its deadline.

## Gameplay Purpose

Hub for 'last good thing' projects and the countdown that structures the game.

## Important NPCs / Encounters

- A Maker tending the atrium greenhouse
- A Goer quietly saying goodbyes

## Possible Events

- Joining a project that won't be finished in time
- The countdown wall loses another day

## Visual / Audio Notes

Dust-gold light through skylights, a single working escalator, distant guitar.
