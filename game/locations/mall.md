# Mall — The Cure Was Love

*Seed: Scenario 1 — The Cure Was Love.*

## Description

A vast dead mall where the Stilled stand frozen at the shops they died wanting something from — a ring counter, a toy store, a photo booth.

## Mood

Cathedral-hushed, lit by skylight and rain. Beautiful and unbearably sad.

## Gameplay Purpose

Hub for low-stakes revivals; teaches the witnessing mechanic among gentle, small griefs.

## Important NPCs / Encounters

- A Stilled bride frozen at the jeweler's window
- A Devoted caretaker who polishes the Stilled like statues

## Possible Events

- A revival that restores a small, sweet memory
- A Stilled who shatters when witnessed too soon

## Visual / Audio Notes

Dripping echoes, a music-box rendition of mall muzak, dust motes in colored light.
