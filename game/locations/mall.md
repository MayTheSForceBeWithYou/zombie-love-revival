# Mall — The Last Town on Earth

*Seed: Scenario 2 — The Last Town on Earth.*

## Description

The repurposed mall is Greenhaven's civic center — town hall in the food court, the Lucid League in the shuttered deli, rationing in the old department store.

## Mood

Fluorescent, bustling, passive-aggressive. Apocalypse bureaucracy with string lights.

## Gameplay Purpose

Main social/political hub; committee meetings, voting drama, and casserole diplomacy.

## Important NPCs / Encounters

- Sable chairing a meeting nobody wants to attend
- Lucid League members organizing in the deli

## Possible Events

- A committee meeting devolves into a romance subplot
- A bulletin-board war goes nuclear

## Visual / Audio Notes

Buzzing fluorescents, overlapping arguments, a PA that only plays smooth jazz.
