# School — The Cure Was Love

*Seed: Scenario 1 — The Cure Was Love.*

## Description

A primary school where childhood griefs froze the youngest Stilled mid-play; the most heartbreaking and the most healable.

## Mood

Tender, haunted, full of small chairs and faded murals.

## Gameplay Purpose

Emotional set-piece location; revivals here grant the largest healing but the deepest ache.

## Important NPCs / Encounters

- A Stilled child clutching a drawing
- Riley, cataloguing the children's lost names

## Possible Events

- Reuniting a Stilled child's drawing with its meaning
- Riley confides how close they are to stilling

## Visual / Audio Notes

Wind through broken windows, a swing creaking, a half-erased chalkboard.
