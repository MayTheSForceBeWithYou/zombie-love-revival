# School — Love at the End of the World

*Seed: Scenario 4 — Love at the End of the World.*

## Description

The school keeps running for the town's last children, who are being given as much ordinary childhood as time allows.

## Mood

Bittersweet warmth; normalcy as an act of defiance.

## Gameplay Purpose

Emotional set-piece about legacy and what's worth passing on.

## Important NPCs / Encounters

- A teacher giving lessons no one will be tested on
- Kids planning a play for the end

## Possible Events

- Helping put on a final school play
- A child asks the player what happens after

## Visual / Audio Notes

Recess noise that means more now, chalk and crayon, a bell rung by hand.
