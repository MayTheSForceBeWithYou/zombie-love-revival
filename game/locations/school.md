# School — The Undead Remember

*Seed: Scenario 3 — The Undead Remember.*

## Description

A school turned learning-center where Rememberers relearn to read, cook, and live — the most visibly hopeful place in town.

## Mood

Bright, kind, quietly triumphant.

## Gameplay Purpose

Restoration set-piece; showcases rebuilding and Riley's recovery arc.

## Important NPCs / Encounters

- Riley relearning skills alongside other Rememberers
- A patient teacher who used to be a Rememberer

## Possible Events

- Riley recovers a joyful memory and shares it
- A class reunion of Rememberers and the families who waited

## Visual / Audio Notes

Children's-book read-alouds, chalk dust, a piano someone is relearning.
