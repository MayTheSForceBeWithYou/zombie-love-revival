# School — The Last Town on Earth

*Seed: Scenario 2 — The Last Town on Earth.*

## Description

The school gym hosts every town vote, dance, and shouting match — folding chairs, a banner, and democracy at its most exhausting.

## Mood

Civic theater; equal parts hopeful and farcical.

## Gameplay Purpose

Set-piece for town votes and big public romance/political beats.

## Important NPCs / Encounters

- A self-important poll volunteer
- Crow emceeing with a hand-held mic

## Possible Events

- A debate where the player is unexpectedly nominated
- A town dance where a cross-faction romance goes public

## Visual / Audio Notes

Squeaky sneakers on gym floor, feedback from a bad mic, crepe-paper decorations.
