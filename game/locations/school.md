# School — The Monster You Choose to Become

*Seed: Scenario 5 — The Monster You Choose to Become.*

## Description

An occupied school turned Choir sanctuary, classrooms converted to shrines and the gym to a ritual hall painted floor to ceiling in glyphs.

## Mood

Feverish reverence; beautiful and a little terrifying.

## Gameplay Purpose

Choir faction hub and Riley's route; the worship-vs-love question lives here.

## Important NPCs / Encounters

- Riley leading a devotional
- A child convert the player feels responsible for

## Possible Events

- A ritual that treats the player as divine
- Riley asks the player to accept what the Choir believes

## Visual / Audio Notes

Chanting, censer smoke, chalk glyphs glowing under blacklight, a single struck gong.
