# Bittersweet Ending — Love at the End of the World

*Seed: Scenario 4 — Love at the End of the World.*

## Ending Summary

The player and a chosen few leave to spend the final days somewhere new, trading the town's legacy for a small, fierce, private happiness at the end.

## Requirements

- Chose to leave together in Event 003
- Strong single romance
- Mixed legacy/personal choices

## Emotional Tone

Tender and uncertain; a small light against a vast dark.

## Final Scene Concept

A car at the end of the road at dawn, the town behind them, the player and their love driving toward whatever last days remain — together, which is the only thing they wanted.

## What Happens To Each Character

- **Player:** Spends the end on their own terms, beside the one they chose.
- **Sable:** If romanced, leaves the project as a gift to the town and goes with the player.
- **Riley:** If romanced, shares the road for as long as they last.
- **Crow:** Stays behind to keep the book and wave them off.
