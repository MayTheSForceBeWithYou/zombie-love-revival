# Bittersweet Ending — The Last Town on Earth

*Seed: Scenario 2 — The Last Town on Earth.*

## Ending Summary

The town survives but stays divided; a fragile truce holds, and the player's romance endures privately even as the bigger dream of true coexistence is deferred.

## Requirements

- Brokered compromise without full coexistence
- Maintained one strong romance
- Handled the conspiracy quietly

## Emotional Tone

Wry, hopeful-tired, realistic.

## Final Scene Concept

Two caucuses sit on opposite sides of the gym, but the player and partner share a quiet exit together, and Crow leaves the string lights on 'for next time.'

## What Happens To Each Character

- **Player:** Stays, choosing one good relationship over fixing everything.
- **Sable:** Holds the town together by sheer will, a little less alone.
- **Riley:** Keeps organizing; the dream deferred, not dead.
- **Crow:** Keeps broadcasting, keeps hoping, keeps the bit going.
