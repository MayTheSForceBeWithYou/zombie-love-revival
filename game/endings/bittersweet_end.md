# Bittersweet Ending — The Monster You Choose to Become

*Seed: Scenario 5 — The Monster You Choose to Become.*

## Ending Summary

The player rejects both savior and destroyer and walks away as something with no name — free, but apart from everyone who wanted to define them.

## Requirements

- Chose the 'something new' path
- Kept self-determination over either faction
- Refused both worship and the Cordon

## Emotional Tone

Lonely, defiant, strangely hopeful — freedom at a cost.

## Final Scene Concept

The player leaves the city at dawn, neither god nor monster, carrying their single-letter name into the unknown. Crow's last broadcast lets the legend stay unfinished, on purpose.

## What Happens To Each Character

- **Player:** Becomes wholly new and walks their own road, self-defined and alone.
- **Sable:** If romanced, is asked to come and must choose; the ending leaves it open.
- **Riley:** Learns the hardest lesson — that the player was never theirs to define.
- **Crow:** Refuses to write the ending, leaving the myth open for whoever comes next.
