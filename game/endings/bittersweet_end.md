# Bittersweet Ending — The Undead Remember

*Seed: Scenario 3 — The Undead Remember.*

## Ending Summary

The player leaves some truths buried, choosing peace over completeness; the town heals partially and the player accepts they may never know everything about who they were.

## Requirements

- Left at least one dangerous memory unrecovered
- Moderate-to-high romance
- Let Crow keep the player's final memory

## Emotional Tone

Gentle melancholy; healing without full closure.

## Final Scene Concept

One blank space remains on the wall, by choice. The player and their partner plant something in the memorial garden and decide that who they are now is enough.

## What Happens To Each Character

- **Player:** Choose to live unburdened by a past they let lie.
- **Sable:** Keeps one secret kindly, and keeps living.
- **Riley:** Recovers enough to be themselves, leaves one door closed.
- **Crow:** Keeps the unopened memory safe, just in case, forever.
