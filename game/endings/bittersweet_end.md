# Bittersweet Ending — The Cure Was Love

*Seed: Scenario 1 — The Cure Was Love.*

## Ending Summary

The player makes peace with the Devoted, choosing to preserve rather than revive, and lets the dead rest while keeping themselves intact for the living.

## Requirements

- Sided with or reconciled the Devoted in Event 003
- Moderate romance
- Mixed revival/preservation choices

## Emotional Tone

Melancholy acceptance; grief honored rather than undone.

## Final Scene Concept

Candles among the Stilled in the cemetery; the player and their partner tend the dead like a garden, choosing the living world over an impossible rescue.

## What Happens To Each Character

- **Player:** Intact, changed, learning to let the dead be dead.
- **Sable:** Finds uneasy peace tending the town and the Stilled.
- **Riley:** Allowed to gently still, remembered and at rest, if their route went this way.
- **Crow:** Becomes the keeper of the cemetery's names, content.
