# Bad Ending — The Undead Remember

*Seed: Scenario 3 — The Undead Remember.*

## Ending Summary

The player forces every truth into the light regardless of cost; recovered traumas shatter the Rememberers and the player's own past breaks them.

## Requirements

- Always chose truth over kindness
- Pushed dangerous recoveries
- Recovered the player's memory without being ready

## Emotional Tone

Tragic; knowledge as a wound that won't close.

## Final Scene Concept

An emptying town as Rememberers regress under memories they couldn't carry. The player sits before a full wall of names, remembering everything and unable to forgive any of it.

## What Happens To Each Character

- **Player:** Crushed by total recall, including their role in the Fall.
- **Sable:** Vindicated and alone, her warnings ignored.
- **Riley:** Regresses to blankness under a memory too heavy.
- **Crow:** Mourns that he returned the memory at all.
