# Bad Ending — The Cure Was Love

*Seed: Scenario 1 — The Cure Was Love.*

## Ending Summary

The player spends everything reviving the dead and erases themselves entirely, becoming a nameless Listener who heals strangers but remembers no one.

## Requirements

- Paid every memory cost
- Neglected both romances
- Chose to heal over self-preservation at every turn

## Emotional Tone

Quietly devastating; a tragedy of selfless love taken too far.

## Final Scene Concept

The town is full of revived, weeping people thanking a stranger who doesn't know why they're crying too. The player walks on to the next grief, the last letter of their name already gone.

## What Happens To Each Character

- **Player:** Fully erased — a kind, hollow Listener with no past.
- **Sable:** Lives, but mourns the person who saved everyone but themselves.
- **Riley:** Either lost to stilling or revived but grieving a partner who no longer knows them.
- **Crow:** Keeps the player's story alone, the last to remember they ever existed.
