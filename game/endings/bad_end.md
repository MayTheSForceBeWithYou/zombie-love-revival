# Bad Ending — Love at the End of the World

*Seed: Scenario 4 — Love at the End of the World.*

## Ending Summary

The player spends the end in denial and isolation; the town scatters in fear and the final days are wasted in bitterness and avoidance.

## Requirements

- Stayed a Holdout, refusing the count
- Neglected relationships
- Abandoned the town's last projects

## Emotional Tone

Cold and lonely; a tragedy of wasted time.

## Final Scene Concept

The player alone at the empty clifftop house as the light fails, surrounded by unfinished things and unsaid words, the countdown wall reading zero.

## What Happens To Each Character

- **Player:** Meets the end alone, regretting the season they wasted.
- **Sable:** Dies with her project unfinished and her feelings unsaid.
- **Riley:** Fades unaccompanied, the goodbye never shared.
- **Crow:** Turns off the light with no one left to keep it for.
