# Bad Ending — The Monster You Choose to Become

*Seed: Scenario 5 — The Monster You Choose to Become.*

## Ending Summary

The player embraces the destroyer path; the old world ends in fire and worship, and even those who loved the player are consumed by what they became.

## Requirements

- Chose the destroyer path at the Becoming
- Fed the cult, broke Sable's loyalty
- Used power to dominate

## Emotional Tone

Apocalyptic and tragic; power without restraint.

## Final Scene Concept

The city burns under glyph-lit skies as the dead rise to the player's will. Crow's final broadcast is the eulogy of the old world — and a warning.

## What Happens To Each Character

- **Player:** Becomes something terrible and unrecognizable, worshipped and alone.
- **Sable:** Dies opposing the player, or is consumed refusing to.
- **Riley:** Devoured by their own faith in what the player became.
- **Crow:** Broadcasts the end, realizing too late which ending he wrote toward.
