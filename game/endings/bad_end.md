# Bad Ending — The Last Town on Earth

*Seed: Scenario 2 — The Last Town on Earth.*

## Ending Summary

The town fractures along faction lines; the Vote collapses into recrimination, and the player's relationships are spent as political ammunition until nothing's left.

## Requirements

- Played factions against each other
- Buried the conspiracy for leverage
- Neglected romances

## Emotional Tone

Bitterly comic curdling into real loss.

## Final Scene Concept

An empty gym, folding chairs knocked over, the bulletin board stripped bare. Crow signs off his radio show for the last time as the lights go out on Greenhaven.

## What Happens To Each Character

- **Player:** Leaves town alone, just another Rover again.
- **Sable:** Loses the town she gave everything to.
- **Riley:** Loses legal personhood and drifts back among the unlucid dead.
- **Crow:** Goes quiet — the worst sign of all.
