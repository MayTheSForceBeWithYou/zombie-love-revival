# Good Ending — The Last Town on Earth

*Seed: Scenario 2 — The Last Town on Earth.*

## Ending Summary

The Vote passes a true coexistence charter; the living and Lucids share the town, and the player's chosen romance becomes the town's first cross-line couple.

## Requirements

- Backed coexistence consistently
- High romance with Sable or Riley
- Exposed or resolved the rationing conspiracy honestly

## Emotional Tone

Warm, earned, comedically triumphant.

## Final Scene Concept

A chaotic, joyful town hall where the charter passes and someone immediately proposes a new committee about it. The player and partner share a kiss as Crow narrates to the whole town over the PA.

## What Happens To Each Character

- **Player:** Becomes a respected (and overcommitted) town fixture who finally chose to stay.
- **Sable:** If romanced, lets the town see her heart and keeps leading, lighter; otherwise keeps the peace.
- **Riley:** If romanced, gains legal personhood and a partner; opens the best soup kitchen in town.
- **Crow:** Gets his own radio hour and the last word, as always.
