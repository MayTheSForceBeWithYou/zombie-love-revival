# Good Ending — The Monster You Choose to Become

*Seed: Scenario 5 — The Monster You Choose to Become.*

## Ending Summary

The player becomes a true bridge between living and dead — a protector who proves transformation isn't damnation, earning the trust of the survivors and the devotion of the dead.

## Requirements

- Chose the savior path at the Becoming
- Won Sable's loyalty and tempered Riley's worship into love
- Used power to protect, not dominate

## Emotional Tone

Triumphant and defiant; a new myth with a hopeful ending.

## Final Scene Concept

The player stands between a leveled Cordon gun and a kneeling crowd of the dead and refuses both fear and worship, founding something new. Crow broadcasts the legend of the one who chose to protect.

## What Happens To Each Character

- **Player:** Becomes a new kind of being and a willing guardian of both worlds.
- **Sable:** If romanced, defects fully and stands beside the player; otherwise lays down arms.
- **Riley:** If romanced, learns to love the person, not just the myth.
- **Crow:** Spreads the true legend, his steering finally aimed at hope.
