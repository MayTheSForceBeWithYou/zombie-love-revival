# Good Ending — Love at the End of the World

*Seed: Scenario 4 — Love at the End of the World.*

## Ending Summary

The town meets the end together with grace, having spent its last season building meaning and love; nothing is saved, but everything matters.

## Requirements

- Committed to a 'last good thing'
- Deep romance or full legacy route
- Helped others meet the end with dignity

## Emotional Tone

Bittersweet and luminous; peace at the end of everything.

## Final Scene Concept

A final shared sunset on the clifftop, the whole town gathered, Crow's book full of good days. The player and the people they love watch the last light together, unafraid.

## What Happens To Each Character

- **Player:** Spends the end surrounded by love, at peace.
- **Sable:** If romanced, finishes her project and faces the end hand in hand with the player.
- **Riley:** Fades gently, having taught the player how to let go.
- **Crow:** Closes his book and keeps the light on to the last page.
