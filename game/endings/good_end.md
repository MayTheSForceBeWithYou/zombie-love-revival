# Good Ending — The Undead Remember

*Seed: Scenario 3 — The Undead Remember.*

## Ending Summary

The player recovers the town's lost memories and their own, and uses the truth to forgive rather than condemn, healing the community and themselves.

## Requirements

- Recovered key memories with care
- High romance built on shared recovery
- Chose forgiveness when the truth was hard

## Emotional Tone

Hopeful, restorative, quietly joyful.

## Final Scene Concept

The wall of recovered names is full, including the player's. The town gathers in the memorial garden as the last Rememberer is welcomed home, and the player chooses to live with the truth rather than be ruled by it.

## What Happens To Each Character

- **Player:** Whole and remembered, at peace with their past role.
- **Sable:** If romanced, finally sets down her perfect memory and lives lighter; otherwise becomes the town's gentle historian.
- **Riley:** If romanced, fully recovers a self they (and you) can love.
- **Crow:** Returns the last held memory and retires as keeper of the wall.
