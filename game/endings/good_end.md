# Good Ending — The Cure Was Love

*Seed: Scenario 1 — The Cure Was Love.*

## Ending Summary

The player saves the one they love without fully erasing themselves, finding a fragile balance between devotion and self-preservation.

## Requirements

- High romance with one partner
- Refused at least one costly revival to preserve self
- Recovered name fragments from Crow

## Emotional Tone

Hard-won, tender, quietly triumphant against a tragic backdrop.

## Final Scene Concept

Dawn over the ruined town; the player, partner, and a few revived souls light candles not for the dead but for the living. The player remembers just enough of themselves to say their partner's name and their own.

## What Happens To Each Character

- **Player:** Keeps a core of self and a recovered partial name; scarred but whole.
- **Sable:** If romanced, finally allows herself to grieve and lives; otherwise keeps the town alive a while longer.
- **Riley:** If romanced and saved, stabilized partway-Stilled but present and loved.
- **Crow:** Returns the player's name and steps back, his vigil complete.
