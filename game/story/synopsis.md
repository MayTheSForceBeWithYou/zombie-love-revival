# Synopsis — The Monster You Choose to Become

*Seed: Scenario 5 — The Monster You Choose to Become.*

## Logline

You're infected — but your mind stayed. As you transform into something neither living nor dead, the survivors start to fear you and the dead start to worship you, and you decide what you become.

## Version-Specific Concept

The player is bitten in the opening hour but keeps their mind. Over the game they evolve into something unprecedented — feared by some, deified by others. In a ruined city of squats, pirate radio, and occult graffiti, the player's choices decide whether they become a savior, a destroyer, or a new kind of being entirely.

## Act Structure

- Act I — The Turn: the player is infected, doesn't die, and is taken in by a crew of scavenger-punks who don't know what to make of them.
- Act II — The Sign: the transformation accelerates; the dead begin to gather, and a cult forms around the player while survivors arm up.
- Act III — The Becoming: the player chooses the shape of what they are — and what that means for everyone who loved or feared them.

## Tone

Punk occult apocalypse. Spray paint, feedback-drenched ritual, scavenger swagger, and the holy terror of a body becoming something new.
