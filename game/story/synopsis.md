# Synopsis — The Last Town on Earth

*Seed: Scenario 2 — The Last Town on Earth.*

## Logline

The last functioning town on Earth runs on potlucks, petty feuds, and a fragile treaty between the living and the polite undead — and you just got assigned a committee seat you absolutely did not want.

## Version-Specific Concept

Greenhaven somehow survived by being too stubborn and too bureaucratic to die. Survivors and 'lucid' zombies coexist under an uneasy treaty enforced mostly by sign-up sheets. The player arrives as an outsider and is immediately drafted into the town's impossible politics, where every romance is also a voting bloc.

## Act Structure

- Act I — New in Town: the player is processed, badged, and assigned to the dreaded Coexistence Committee.
- Act II — Factions & Feelings: navigating the Living Caucus, the Lucid Undead, and the people who fall for you across that line.
- Act III — The Vote: a town-defining referendum where romance, friendship, and conspiracy all come due at once.

## Tone

Dark comedy survival romance. Snark, absurd town politics, awkward zombie courtship, and gallows humor that keeps tipping into real feeling.
