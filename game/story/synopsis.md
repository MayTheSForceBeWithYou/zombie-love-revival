# Synopsis — Love at the End of the World

*Seed: Scenario 4 — Love at the End of the World.*

## Logline

The world already lost. Extinction is a date on a calendar. In the time that's left, the only question that matters is how — and with whom — you choose to spend the end.

## Version-Specific Concept

The apocalypse is over and humanity lost. The remaining survivors and the slow, harmless dead share a dwindling town with a known expiration. Nobody is saving the world. The game is about what you do when you can't — the small, fierce acts of meaning at the end of everything.

## Act Structure

- Act I — The Count: the player arrives and learns the town has roughly one season left before the cold or the quiet takes it.
- Act II — The Days: building relationships and finishing the unfinished business of strangers and self alike.
- Act III — The Last Light: the final days, where every route asks how a life should end and answers differently.

## Tone

Bittersweet and luminous. Quiet, golden-hour melancholy; every moment heavier because it's nearly the last. Tender, never cruel.
