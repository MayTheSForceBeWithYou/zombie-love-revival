# Synopsis — The Undead Remember

*Seed: Scenario 3 — The Undead Remember.*

## Logline

Most of the dead remember nothing — but you can give them their memories back, and every fragment you recover rebuilds a person, a friendship, and a piece of the truth about the night the world ended.

## Version-Specific Concept

Nearly all zombies are blank — no self, no story. A rare few begin to flicker, and the player can enter those flickers and help recover lost memories. Each recovered memory restores a measure of personhood, reveals a clue about the outbreak, and unearths fragments of the player's own forgotten past.

## Act Structure

- Act I — The First Flicker: the player discovers their gift by waking the first remembering zombie and begins rebuilding the safehouse community.
- Act II — The Archive: assembling recovered memories into a shared picture, while romance grows through exploring each other's pasts.
- Act III — The Night It Happened: the final memories converge on the outbreak's origin — and the player's role in it.

## Tone

Hopeful and restorative with a mystery's pull. Quiet wonder, the ache of recovered memory, and the slow joy of rebuilding.
