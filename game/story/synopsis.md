# Synopsis — The Cure Was Love

*Seed: Scenario 1 — The Cure Was Love.*

## Logline

In a town where grief literally turns people undead, the only cure is being truly seen — and love is a force that can either revive the dead or finish killing them.

## Version-Specific Concept

The outbreak was never a virus. It was emotional collapse — loneliness, regret, and unprocessed grief crystallizing the body into the undead. The zombies are not gone; they are trapped inside the worst moment of their lives, replaying it. The player is a rare 'Listener' who can reach them. Each act of genuine connection can thaw a wound or freeze it harder.

## Act Structure

- Act I — Arrival: the player wakes in Millhaven with a single-letter name and discovers they can speak to the 'Stilled' (the local undead).
- Act II — Devotion: deepening bonds with Sable and Riley while learning that every healed soul costs the Listener a piece of their own memory.
- Act III — The Reckoning: the player must choose whom to save when saving everyone would erase the player entirely.

## Tone

Gothic romantic tragedy. Ruined beauty, candle-light, devotion, and poetic darkness. Tender but unflinching about loss.
