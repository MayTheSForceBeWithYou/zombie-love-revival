# World — Love at the End of the World

*Seed: Scenario 4 — Love at the End of the World.*

## World State

A coastal town at the literal end of the road, beautiful and winding down. Power flickers, gardens go to seed, and the dead drift harmlessly along the shore. Everyone knows the date. Most have made peace with it; some haven't.

## How the Outbreak Works Here

The infection won, then stalled — the dead are slow, gentle, and fading like everyone else. There is no cure and no point pretending otherwise. The threat now is despair, not teeth.

## Central Conflict

Not survival, but meaning. With limited days, the player must decide where to spend them: with whom, on what, and whether to spend the end fighting it, accepting it, or making it beautiful.

## Factions & Forces

- The Makers — people building one last good thing (a garden, a concert, a child's birthday) before the end.
- The Goers — those who want to leave on their own terms, on their own schedule.
- The Holdouts — survivors still pretending rescue is coming, and the kindness or cruelty of letting them.
