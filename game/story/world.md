# World — The Cure Was Love

*Seed: Scenario 1 — The Cure Was Love.*

## World State

Millhaven is a candle-lit ruin reclaimed by ivy and rain. The living are few and the Stilled stand frozen mid-gesture on every corner, like statues of their own heartbreak. Beauty and decay are inseparable here.

## How the Outbreak Works Here

There is no pathogen. When a person's grief exceeds what they can hold, the body stills and the mind loops inside one unbearable memory. A Listener can step into that memory and witness it. Being witnessed is the only thing that lets a Stilled move forward — or shatter.

## Central Conflict

Healing the dead is real, but it is not free. Each revival draws on the Listener's own emotional reserves and erodes their memories. To save the town the player may have to forget the very people they came to love.

## Factions & Forces

- The Listeners — a near-extinct order who can enter the grief of the Stilled; the player is the last initiated one.
- The Wardens — survivors who believe the Stilled should be burned, not spoken to; led in spirit by the town's fear.
- The Devoted — a quiet cult that tends the Stilled like a garden, preserving them rather than healing them.
