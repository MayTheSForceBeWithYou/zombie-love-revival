# World — The Undead Remember

*Seed: Scenario 3 — The Undead Remember.*

## World State

A quiet, green-overgrown town slowly being reclaimed by the living and the remembering. The mood is dawn, not dusk: gardens in old parking lots, a returning hum of community, a wall of recovered names.

## How the Outbreak Works Here

The infection erases memory first and the body second. Recovery runs in reverse: when a Listener helps a zombie reclaim a true memory, the person comes partway back. The deeper the memory, the closer to the truth of what caused the Fall.

## Central Conflict

Not every memory should be recovered. Some truths heal; others destroy the person who remembers — and the player's own buried past may be the most dangerous memory of all.

## Factions & Forces

- The Rememberers — recovering zombies and the survivors helping them rebuild.
- The Archivists — survivors who catalogue every recovered memory, obsessed with the truth at any cost.
- The Let-Lie — those who believe the dead should stay blank and the past should stay buried.
