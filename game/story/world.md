# World — The Monster You Choose to Become

*Seed: Scenario 5 — The Monster You Choose to Become.*

## World State

A blacked-out city reclaimed by scavenger crews, plastered in glyphs and gig flyers. Generators throb, pirate radio crackles, and shrines to 'the New Ones' bloom in the underpasses. It's loud, defiant, and very much alive in the dark.

## How the Outbreak Works Here

The infection rewrites you, but rarely leaves the mind. The player is one of the vanishingly few who turn while staying themselves — and keep changing, gaining strange abilities and a pull the dead can feel. Some call it disease; the cults call it ascension.

## Central Conflict

The player is becoming something with real power and no precedent. Survivors want it contained, cults want it enthroned, and the player must decide what to do with a body and a following that could remake or end the world.

## Factions & Forces

- The Static — scavenger-punk crews who run pirate radio and trust no gospel, including the one forming around the player.
- The Choir of New Ones — an occult movement that worships the lucid infected as the next step.
- The Cordon — surviving militia determined to put down anything that isn't fully human, player included.
