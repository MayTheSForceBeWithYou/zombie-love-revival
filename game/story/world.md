# World — The Last Town on Earth

*Seed: Scenario 2 — The Last Town on Earth.*

## World State

Greenhaven is the last lit town for a hundred miles: string lights, ration coupons, a weaponized HOA, and a cemetery that throws better parties than the living. The undead here are slow but courteous, and everyone has Opinions.

## How the Outbreak Works Here

The outbreak burned out years ago. What's left are 'Lucids' — zombies who plateaued with enough mind to hold a grudge, fill out a form, and occasionally fall in love. They don't eat people; they mostly just want to be invited to things.

## Central Conflict

Two factions want incompatible futures for the town, and the player's relationships keep getting weaponized as political capital. Everyone wants you on their side; nobody asks what you want.

## Factions & Forces

- The Living Caucus — pro-human-priority, runs the rations, deeply petty.
- The Lucid League — undead civil-rights bloc that meets in the deli after it closes.
- The Neutrals — vendors, gossips, and the player's only honest information source, led by Crow.
