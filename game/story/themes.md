# Themes — The Last Town on Earth

*Seed: Scenario 2 — The Last Town on Earth.*

## Core Themes

- Found family
- Community vs. self
- Trust across difference
- Civilization as a series of awkward meetings
- Love as politics

## Central Question

What do you owe a community that drafted you, fed you, and won't stop arguing about you?

## Recurring Motifs

- Sign-up sheets and clipboards
- Casserole diplomacy
- Passive-aggressive bulletin boards
- Folding chairs in a school gym
