# Themes — The Monster You Choose to Become

*Seed: Scenario 5 — The Monster You Choose to Become.*

## Core Themes

- Transformation
- Identity and self-determination
- Fear and the other
- Power and what you do with it
- Rebellion

## Central Question

When everyone else is telling you what you are, who gets to decide what you become?

## Recurring Motifs

- Spray-painted glyphs that 'work'
- Feedback and pirate radio
- Shrines in underpasses
- A body changing in the mirror
