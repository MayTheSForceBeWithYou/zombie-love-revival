# Themes — The Cure Was Love

*Seed: Scenario 1 — The Cure Was Love.*

## Core Themes

- Grief as a living thing
- Redemption
- Empathy as power
- The cost of devotion
- Memory and forgetting

## Central Question

Is it loving to bring someone back if you have to lose yourself to do it?

## Recurring Motifs

- Candles guttering in rain
- Frozen mid-gesture statues
- Pressed flowers in unsent letters
- The sound of a held breath
