# Romance Routes — The Monster You Choose to Become

*Seed: Scenario 5 — The Monster You Choose to Become.*

## Overview

Each romance is a different answer to the player's transformation: one fears it and stays anyway, one believes in it, and the tension of being loved while becoming unrecognizable drives every route.

## Route Ideas

- Sable route — 'Loaded Question': a Cordon-adjacent survivor who should put the player down and falls for them instead; love against every instinct.
- Riley route — 'True Believer': a Choir member who loves what the player is becoming, forcing the player to ask if they're loved or worshipped.
- Self route — 'The Becoming': the relationship with one's own changing body and mind, with companions as witnesses to who you choose to be.

## How Crow Frames the Story

Crow is a mythic messenger and pirate-radio prophet, narrating the player's transformation like an unfolding legend — half trickster hype-man, half oracle who may know how the story ends.
