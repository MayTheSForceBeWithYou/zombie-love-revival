# Romance Routes — The Undead Remember

*Seed: Scenario 3 — The Undead Remember.*

## Overview

Romance grows through shared memory work. Falling in love means helping someone reassemble who they were — and being willing to be reassembled in return, flaws and all.

## Route Ideas

- Sable route — 'The Witness': a living survivor who remembers everything and wishes she didn't; healing her means letting her finally set it down.
- Riley route — 'The Returning': romancing a remembering zombie as they recover, never sure which version of them you'll meet tomorrow.
- Mutual-recovery route — late-game path where the player and a partner recover a shared memory of who they were to each other before the Fall.

## How Crow Frames the Story

Crow is the gentle archivist-guide, narrating like someone reading aloud from a recovered journal — curious, kind, and quietly steering the player toward (or away from) certain truths.
