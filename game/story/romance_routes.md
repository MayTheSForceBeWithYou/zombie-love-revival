# Romance Routes — Love at the End of the World

*Seed: Scenario 4 — Love at the End of the World.*

## Overview

Every romance is finite and knows it. The question each route poses is not 'will we last' but 'how do we want to spend what's left' — making the small choices feel enormous.

## Route Ideas

- Sable route — 'One Good Season': falling for the woman determined to build something meaningful before the end, even knowing it can't last.
- Riley route — 'The Long Goodbye': a romance with one of the gentle dead, learning to love something already leaving.
- Solo/legacy route — choosing to spend the end caring for the whole town rather than one person; quietly devastating and quietly noble.

## How Crow Frames the Story

Crow is the melancholic witness who narrates like he's already a little bit gone — wry, tender, marking the days and reminding the player that being remembered is the only afterlife on offer.
