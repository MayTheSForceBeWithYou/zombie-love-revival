# Romance Routes — The Last Town on Earth

*Seed: Scenario 2 — The Last Town on Earth.*

## Overview

Romance and town politics are the same system. Each love interest is also a constituency, and pursuing them shifts the balance of the upcoming Vote — which the game refuses to let you forget.

## Route Ideas

- Sable route — 'Madam Chair': romancing the formidable committee head who runs the town and pretends she has no heart.
- Riley route — 'Across the Aisle': a slow-burn with a Lucid League organizer; charming, dead, and a surprisingly good cook.
- Crow 'friendship route' — not romanceable, but the most reliable relationship in a town of liars.

## How Crow Frames the Story

Crow narrates like the town's unofficial talk-radio host — fast, funny, dropping gossip and the occasional gut-punch of sincerity when the bit gets too real.
