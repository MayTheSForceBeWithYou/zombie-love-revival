# Romance Routes — The Cure Was Love

*Seed: Scenario 1 — The Cure Was Love.*

## Overview

Both romances are about being truly witnessed. Sable and Riley are living, but each carries a grief that could still them. Love is presented as the same force that heals the dead — dangerous, holy, and never safe.

## Route Ideas

- Sable route — 'The Vigil': falling for the woman keeping the town alive while she quietly grieves the people she could not save.
- Riley route — 'The Letter': a tender, doomed-feeling romance with someone who is already half-Stilled and fading.
- Optional 'Stilled' romance — reviving a specific beloved Stilled person, the most tragic and costly path.

## How Crow Frames the Story

Crow is the narrator-elegist. He speaks in hushed, poetic asides, naming each loss like a funeral rite. He never intervenes; he only witnesses and remembers, so that when the player forgets, someone still holds the story.
