# Riley — Love at the End of the World

*Seed: Scenario 4 — Love at the End of the World. Branch: characterAndStoryBrainstorming-v4.*

## Role in Story

Romanceable, one of the gentle dead. Fading slowly, lucid in flashes, sweet and unafraid; teaches the player how to say goodbye well.

## Personality

- Serene and kind
- Unafraid of the end
- Playful in their good hours
- Wise about letting go

## Visual Concept

Translucent pallor, a faded festival shirt, sea-glass in their pockets; calm eyes that seem to look a little past you.

## Relationship to the Player

A romance that is a long, beautiful goodbye — loving someone who is already partway gone, and being okay with it.

## Secrets / Emotional Wounds

- They've accepted the end completely and worry the player hasn't
- They're losing more of themselves each day
- They want to give the player permission to grieve

## Possible Route Arc

- Gentle companionship → tender romance → fading → teaching the player how to let go

## Sample Dialogue

> I'm not scared. That surprised me too.

> Collect the good hours. Don't grieve the ones I'm not in yet.

> (softly) When I'm mostly gone, you're allowed to keep living. Promise me.
