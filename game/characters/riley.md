# Riley — The Cure Was Love

*Seed: Scenario 1 — The Cure Was Love. Branch: characterAndStoryBrainstorming-v1.*

## Role in Story

Romanceable human, slowly going Stilled. A gentle archivist of the town's lost lives who is fading at the edges.

## Personality

- Warm and curious
- Romantic in a doomed register
- Quietly resigned
- Finds beauty in ruin

## Visual Concept

Soft features, ink-stained fingers, a coat full of other people's photographs. A faint pallor and stillness creeps in as their route progresses.

## Relationship to the Player

The most reciprocal romance — and the one most likely to end in loss. Loving Riley means racing their own stilling.

## Secrets / Emotional Wounds

- They are already partway Stilled and hiding it
- They have been quietly cataloguing the player's vanishing memories
- They want to be remembered more than they want to be saved

## Possible Route Arc

- Companionship → confession → the player learns Riley is fading → final choice to revive Riley at great cost or let them go gently

## Sample Dialogue

> I keep everyone's photographs so someone holds them.

> Don't save me if it costs you a memory of me. That's the deal.

> (fading) Read me back to myself. Just once more.
