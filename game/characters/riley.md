# Riley — The Last Town on Earth

*Seed: Scenario 2 — The Last Town on Earth. Branch: characterAndStoryBrainstorming-v2.*

## Role in Story

Romanceable Lucid (undead) human. A Lucid League organizer with great manners, a slow heartbeat, and the best soup in town.

## Personality

- Earnest and warm
- Self-deprecating about being dead
- Idealistic
- Awkwardly romantic

## Visual Concept

Cardigan over visible stitches, a 'kiss the cook (gently)' apron, careful well-rehearsed smile that hides missing teeth.

## Relationship to the Player

The sweetest, weirdest romance — courtship across the living/undead line, complete with the town watching.

## Secrets / Emotional Wounds

- Remembers their human death and hasn't told anyone
- Is faking how 'lucid' they are on bad days
- Organized the Lucid League partly to meet someone

## Possible Route Arc

- Friendly outreach → mutual crush → the town's prejudice tests them → a referendum that decides their legal personhood and the romance both

## Sample Dialogue

> I made soup. It's the one thing my hands still remember how to do right.

> Dating a dead guy is a choice. I just want it to be a good one.

> (soft) If the Vote goes wrong, I lose my name on paper. Will you still know it?
