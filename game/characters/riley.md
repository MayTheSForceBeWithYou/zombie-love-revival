# Riley — The Monster You Choose to Become

*Seed: Scenario 5 — The Monster You Choose to Become. Branch: characterAndStoryBrainstorming-v5.*

## Role in Story

Romanceable human. A Choir true-believer who sees the player as the New One made flesh; devout, intense, in love with the transformation.

## Personality

- Fervent and warm
- Idealistic to a fault
- Tender
- Dangerously certain

## Visual Concept

Hand-painted glyph robes over thrift-store punk, a censer of scavenged incense, eyes shining with belief.

## Relationship to the Player

The thrilling, troubling route — being adored for what you're becoming, and never sure if it's love or worship.

## Secrets / Emotional Wounds

- Their faith is partly a cope for a grief they won't name
- They'd let the player consume them if asked
- They fear being loved back as just a person

## Possible Route Arc

- Devotee → lover → forced to separate the player from the myth → chooses faith or the person

## Sample Dialogue

> You're not sick. You're the first page of something new.

> I'd follow what you're becoming anywhere. I'd follow you even further.

> (uncertain) Do you want me, or do you want a believer? ...Do I?
