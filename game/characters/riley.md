# Riley — The Undead Remember

*Seed: Scenario 3 — The Undead Remember. Branch: characterAndStoryBrainstorming-v3.*

## Role in Story

Romanceable, a remembering zombie. Recovering personhood one fragment at a time; warm, funny, and frightened of forgetting again.

## Personality

- Gentle and hopeful
- Disoriented but trying
- Delighted by small recovered joys
- Scared of regression

## Visual Concept

Soft-eyed, scarred, wearing clothes that don't quite fit the person they're becoming; a string bracelet they don't yet remember receiving.

## Relationship to the Player

The most tender route — loving someone who is literally becoming themselves again, never sure who they'll wake as.

## Secrets / Emotional Wounds

- They knew the player before turning
- Some of their recovered memories are of harming others
- They're terrified the 'real' them was not a good person

## Possible Route Arc

- First flicker → companionship → recovering their identity → facing a memory that could undo all the healing

## Sample Dialogue

> I remembered the taste of rain today. I cried. I think that's normal?

> Every morning I'm a little more me. I just hope you like who that turns out to be.

> (afraid) What if I remember something that makes you leave?
