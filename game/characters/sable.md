# Sable — The Last Town on Earth

*Seed: Scenario 2 — The Last Town on Earth. Branch: characterAndStoryBrainstorming-v2.*

## Role in Story

Romanceable human. Chair of the Coexistence Committee; runs Greenhaven on spite, spreadsheets, and a heart she denies owning.

## Personality

- Imperious
- Secretly overwhelmed
- Brutally funny
- Fiercely protective of 'her' town

## Visual Concept

Blazer over body armor, clipboard as a scepter, reading glasses she uses to glare over.

## Relationship to the Player

Antagonistic banter sliding into respect and heat. Romancing her means seeing the exhaustion behind the gavel.

## Secrets / Emotional Wounds

- She's been quietly covering a rationing shortfall
- She used to be a wedding planner and hates that it's useful
- She likes the Lucids more than she'll admit

## Possible Route Arc

- Bulldozes the player → recruits them → softens → must choose between power and the player at the Vote

## Sample Dialogue

> Welcome to Greenhaven. Here's your lanyard. Don't lose it or yourself.

> I don't have favorites. You're my least-disappointing problem.

> (tired) I planned weddings before all this. Now I plan funerals and zoning. Romantic, right?
