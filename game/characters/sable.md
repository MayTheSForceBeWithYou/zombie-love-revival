# Sable — Love at the End of the World

*Seed: Scenario 4 — Love at the End of the World. Branch: characterAndStoryBrainstorming-v4.*

## Role in Story

Romanceable human. A builder racing the clock to leave something good behind; refuses to let the end be ugly.

## Personality

- Driven and warm
- Defiantly hopeful
- Exhausted but unbroken
- Romantic about small things

## Visual Concept

Dirt under her nails, blueprint rolled under one arm, a smile she's chosen to keep; a coat patched many times.

## Relationship to the Player

Romance as shared purpose — building one last good thing together and learning to call that enough.

## Secrets / Emotional Wounds

- She's sicker than she's told anyone
- The 'project' is a memorial she hasn't named
- She's afraid of dying with nothing finished

## Possible Route Arc

- Recruits the player into a project → falls for them → races to finish it → chooses how to face the end together

## Sample Dialogue

> We can't save it. We can still make it beautiful on the way out.

> I'd rather build something that won't last than nothing at all.

> (quiet) I want one good season. Spend it with me?
