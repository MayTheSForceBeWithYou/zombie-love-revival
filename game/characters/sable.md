# Sable — The Undead Remember

*Seed: Scenario 3 — The Undead Remember. Branch: characterAndStoryBrainstorming-v3.*

## Role in Story

Romanceable human. A survivor cursed with perfect memory of the Fall; she runs the safehouse and carries everything no one else can.

## Personality

- Steady and grounded
- Burdened by total recall
- Dryly kind
- Quietly breaking under what she remembers

## Visual Concept

Weathered jacket, a journal she never opens, eyes that look like they've reread the same bad day a thousand times.

## Relationship to the Player

Romance as relief — helping her finally set down a memory she's carried alone. She resists being 'fixed.'

## Secrets / Emotional Wounds

- She remembers the player from before the Fall
- She's been hiding a memory that implicates someone they both love
- She envies the blankness she helps others escape

## Possible Route Arc

- Guarded mentor → confidante → the choice to share the memory that changes everything

## Sample Dialogue

> I remember all of it. That's not a gift. That's a sentence.

> Don't recover me. There's nothing in here you'll thank me for.

> (finally) I remember you. From before. I've been deciding whether to tell you for months.
