# Sable — The Cure Was Love

*Seed: Scenario 1 — The Cure Was Love. Branch: characterAndStoryBrainstorming-v1.*

## Role in Story

Romanceable human. The town's exhausted keeper, who has buried everyone she loved and refuses to grieve in case grief stills her too.

## Personality

- Precise and controlled
- Privately tender
- Terrified of her own sorrow
- Loyal to the point of self-destruction

## Visual Concept

Lantern-jawed and lantern-carrying; ash-grey coat, calloused hands, a ledger of the dead she never lets anyone read.

## Relationship to the Player

Wary, then fiercely protective. She fears the player will spend themselves into nothing — because she would do the same.

## Secrets / Emotional Wounds

- She caused a death she has never confessed
- She is closer to being Stilled than anyone knows
- She keeps a Stilled person hidden in her cellar

## Possible Route Arc

- Refuses help → tests the player → lets herself grieve → either thaws or stills depending on the player's care

## Sample Dialogue

> I don't cry. I can't afford the weather.

> You spend yourself on the dead like they'll thank you.

> (breaking) Her name was Mara. I never said it out loud until now.
