# Sable — The Monster You Choose to Become

*Seed: Scenario 5 — The Monster You Choose to Become. Branch: characterAndStoryBrainstorming-v5.*

## Role in Story

Romanceable human. Cordon-trained and lethal; her job is to end things like the player, and she can't make herself do it.

## Personality

- Hard-edged
- Disciplined
- Secretly tender
- At war with her own orders

## Visual Concept

Riot gear repurposed with spray-paint defiance, rifle she keeps slung not aimed, a jaw set against her own feelings.

## Relationship to the Player

Love against every survival instinct. Romancing her means giving her a reason to choose the player over the Cordon.

## Secrets / Emotional Wounds

- She's already disobeyed an order to kill the player
- She's losing faith in the Cordon's certainty
- She's more afraid of loving a monster than fighting one

## Possible Route Arc

- Hunter → reluctant protector → defector → stands beside or against the player at the Becoming

## Sample Dialogue

> My orders are real simple where you're concerned. So why haven't I followed them.

> I've shot things that begged less and looked more human.

> (low) If you turn into something I have to stop, don't make me be the one. Please.
