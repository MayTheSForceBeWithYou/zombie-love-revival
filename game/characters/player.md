# Player — The Undead Remember

*Seed: Scenario 3 — The Undead Remember. Branch: characterAndStoryBrainstorming-v3.*

## Role in Story

A 'Restorer' who can enter and recover lost memories. Renameable, gender-selectable; their own past is the biggest blank of all, marked only by a single remembered letter.

## Personality

- Empathetic and patient
- Insatiably curious
- Afraid of their own missing past
- A natural rebuilder

## Visual Concept

Practical clothes, a satchel of found photographs, a notebook where recovered memories are sketched before they fade.

## Relationship to the Player

The thread that reconnects everyone. Each bond deepens through co-recovering memories, including the player's own.

## Secrets / Emotional Wounds

- Their single-letter name is the only memory they've kept
- They appear in other people's recovered memories — from the night of the Fall
- Recovering their own past may be catastrophic

## Possible Route Arc

- Discover the gift → rebuild the community → confront whether to recover their own role in the outbreak

## Sample Dialogue

> Tell me one true thing you remember. We'll build from there.

> I keep finding myself in other people's memories. I don't know if that's a comfort or a warning.

> (quietly) What if the person I was is someone I won't forgive?
