# Player — The Monster You Choose to Become

*Seed: Scenario 5 — The Monster You Choose to Become. Branch: characterAndStoryBrainstorming-v5.*

## Role in Story

The infected protagonist who kept their mind and won't stop changing. Renameable, gender-selectable; their old name is down to a single letter as the old self burns away.

## Personality

- Defiant
- Increasingly uncanny
- Wrestling with hunger and self
- Magnetic whether they want it or not

## Visual Concept

Patched jacket over skin that's becoming something else — bioluminescent veins, eyes that catch the light wrong; glyphs other people paint on them 'for luck.'

## Relationship to the Player

A gravity well. People orbit, worship, or aim at the player; their choices set the meaning of the transformation.

## Secrets / Emotional Wounds

- They can feel the dead and the dead can feel them
- The single letter is all that's left of who they were
- Part of them likes what they're becoming

## Possible Route Arc

- Turn and survive → discover the pull and the power → choose savior, destroyer, or something wholly new

## Sample Dialogue

> I got bit on day one. I should be shambling. I'm not.

> Everybody's got a name for what I'm turning into. None of them asked me.

> (in the mirror) Whatever I become, I'm choosing it. That part's still mine.
