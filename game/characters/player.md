# Player — The Cure Was Love

*Seed: Scenario 1 — The Cure Was Love. Branch: characterAndStoryBrainstorming-v1.*

## Role in Story

The last Listener. A renameable, gender-selectable protagonist who begins with only a single-letter name because the rest was spent reviving someone they no longer remember.

## Personality

- Quietly devoted
- Haunted by gaps in their own memory
- Drawn toward other people's pain
- Stubbornly hopeful in a hopeless place

## Visual Concept

A traveler in a rain-darkened coat, one glove removed — the bare hand is how they 'touch' grief. Faint frost rimes their fingertips after each revival.

## Relationship to the Player

The fixed point everyone orbits. The player chooses whom to spend themselves on, and the game tracks how much 'self' remains.

## Secrets / Emotional Wounds

- They have already revived someone once and forgot who it was
- Their single-letter name is the last fragment they have of themselves
- Each revival is slowly stilling them too

## Possible Route Arc

- Discover the gift and its price
- Choose whom to give themselves to
- Decide at the end whether to keep their last memory or spend it to save the town

## Sample Dialogue

> I don't remember my name. I remember it had more letters.

> Tell me the worst moment. I'll stand in it with you.

> (softly) If I forget you tomorrow, were we still real today?
