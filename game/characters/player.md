# Player — The Last Town on Earth

*Seed: Scenario 2 — The Last Town on Earth. Branch: characterAndStoryBrainstorming-v2.*

## Role in Story

The newcomer and reluctant swing vote. Renameable and gender-selectable; arrives with a half-remembered single-letter name that the town's intake clerk just writes on a sticker.

## Personality

- Dry, deadpan
- Allergic to meetings
- Secretly a soft touch
- Good in a crisis, terrible with paperwork

## Visual Concept

Travel-worn but freshly issued a too-cheerful 'HELLO MY NAME IS' badge; a backpack that screams 'I did not plan to stay.'

## Relationship to the Player

Everyone's prospective ally and crush. The player's choices nudge the town's factions and the upcoming Vote.

## Secrets / Emotional Wounds

- Doesn't actually remember why they came here
- Is on three committees by accident
- Their single-letter name hides something they're not ready to look at

## Possible Route Arc

- Resist involvement → get hopelessly entangled → choose what kind of town this should be

## Sample Dialogue

> I've been here four hours and I'm on a subcommittee. How.

> I'm not picking a side. ...Okay I picked a side, it was the casserole.

> (quietly) Nobody's asked me to stay anywhere in a long time.
