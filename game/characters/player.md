# Player — Love at the End of the World

*Seed: Scenario 4 — Love at the End of the World. Branch: characterAndStoryBrainstorming-v4.*

## Role in Story

A late arrival to the last town, renameable and gender-selectable, carrying a single-letter name and no plans — because what's the point of plans now?

## Personality

- Wry and tender
- Avoidant of attachment, then all-in
- Quietly searching for meaning
- Afraid of wasting the time that's left

## Visual Concept

Sun-bleached coat, a worn watch they keep checking, a bag of belongings that gets lighter as they give things away.

## Relationship to the Player

The catalyst who decides how the town spends its final season. Each bond is a different answer to 'how should this end?'

## Secrets / Emotional Wounds

- They came here to die alone and keep failing to
- Their single-letter name is the last thing they're holding onto
- They have a list of things to do before the end they can't bring themselves to start

## Possible Route Arc

- Arrive resigned → find reasons to care → choose how to spend the last light

## Sample Dialogue

> No plans. Plans are for people with time.

> I came here to be alone for it. That's going about as well as you'd expect.

> (quietly) If this is the end, I'd rather not spend it pretending I don't care.
