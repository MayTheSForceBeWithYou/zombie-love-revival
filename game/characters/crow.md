# Crow — The Cure Was Love

*Seed: Scenario 1 — The Cure Was Love. Branch: characterAndStoryBrainstorming-v1.*

## Role in Story

Narrator and elegist. A figure who appears at thresholds, names the dead, and frames the story as a long funeral that is also a love letter.

## Personality

- Poetic and hushed
- Compassionate witness
- Refuses to intervene
- Carries every story so none are lost

## Visual Concept

A lean silhouette of black feathers and mourning cloth, seen at the edge of candlelight; eyes like wet ink.

## Relationship to the Player

Guide and confidant. Crow remembers what the player forgets and gently returns fragments when it matters most.

## Secrets / Emotional Wounds

- Crow may be the town's collective grief given a voice
- He cannot be revived or stilled — he simply remembers
- He knew the player's full name once

## Possible Route Arc

- Frames each chapter → withholds the player's true name → decides at the climax whether to give it back

## Sample Dialogue

> Another one stilled at the corner of Vine and Ash. Light a candle; I'll say the name.

> You are forgetting yourself beautifully.

> (at the end) I kept your name safe. Do you want it, or do you want them?
