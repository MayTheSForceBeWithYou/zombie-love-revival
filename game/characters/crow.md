# Crow — The Monster You Choose to Become

*Seed: Scenario 5 — The Monster You Choose to Become. Branch: characterAndStoryBrainstorming-v5.*

## Role in Story

Narrator, mythic messenger, pirate-radio prophet. Broadcasts the legend of the player's becoming across the dark city.

## Personality

- Charismatic
- Trickster-prophet
- Secretly steering events
- Believer in stories over facts

## Visual Concept

Feathers and headphones, a mic strung with charms, a transmitter backpack tagged with his own glyph.

## Relationship to the Player

Hype-man, oracle, and unreliable guide. Crow turns the player's transformation into myth — for good or ill.

## Secrets / Emotional Wounds

- Crow started the Choir's first broadcast as a bit and it became real
- He may know what the player ultimately becomes
- He's collecting the player's story for something only he understands

## Possible Route Arc

- Comic prophet → myth-maker → reveals he's been writing the legend toward a chosen ending

## Sample Dialogue

> Tonight on the only frequency that matters: a dead kid who won't lie down. Stay tuned.

> Gods don't get a say in their own myths. Lucky for you, you're not a god yet.

> (off-air) I've been telling your story since day one. Question is whose ending I'm writing toward.
