# Crow — Love at the End of the World

*Seed: Scenario 4 — Love at the End of the World. Branch: characterAndStoryBrainstorming-v4.*

## Role in Story

Narrator and witness. Marks the passing days, eulogizes the small things, and insists that being remembered is its own kind of survival.

## Personality

- Wry and melancholic
- Tender beneath the jokes
- Already half-resigned
- Devoted to memory

## Visual Concept

A threadbare coat the color of dusk, a chalk stub for the countdown wall, a pocketful of other people's keepsakes.

## Relationship to the Player

Companion and chronicler. Crow keeps the town's record so that someone, somewhere, knows they were here.

## Secrets / Emotional Wounds

- Crow has been keeping a book of everyone's last good day
- He's not sure he'll outlast the town and is at peace with it
- He volunteered to be the last one to turn off the lights

## Possible Route Arc

- Day-marker and comic relief → keeper of legacy → the one who closes the story

## Sample Dialogue

> Day forty-one. The tomatoes came in. Somebody should write that down. I did.

> Being remembered is the only afterlife I've seen evidence for. So. Let's make you memorable.

> (at dusk) I'll keep the light on as long as there's someone to keep it for.
