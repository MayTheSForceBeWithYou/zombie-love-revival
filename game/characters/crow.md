# Crow — The Undead Remember

*Seed: Scenario 3 — The Undead Remember. Branch: characterAndStoryBrainstorming-v3.*

## Role in Story

Narrator and guide. A patient archivist-figure who helps the player interpret recovered memories and decide which to chase.

## Personality

- Calm and kind
- Endlessly curious
- Protective of fragile truths
- Subtly steering

## Visual Concept

A coat full of index cards and tied-string mnemonics, a lantern for the 'dark' memories, ink-stained gloves.

## Relationship to the Player

Mentor and moral compass. Crow knows more about the player's past than he lets on and parcels it out carefully.

## Secrets / Emotional Wounds

- Crow has been preserving the player's memories for them
- He was present at the Fall
- He's not sure the player should ever remember

## Possible Route Arc

- Friendly guide → keeper of the player's past → the one who decides whether to return the final memory

## Sample Dialogue

> Memory is a garden. Some of it you plant; some of it plants you.

> Recover the gentle ones first. Earn the hard ones.

> (carefully) I've been holding a memory for you. When you're ready, not before.
