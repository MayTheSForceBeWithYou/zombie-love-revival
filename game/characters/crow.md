# Crow — The Last Town on Earth

*Seed: Scenario 2 — The Last Town on Earth. Branch: characterAndStoryBrainstorming-v2.*

## Role in Story

Narrator, trickster, and town gossip-in-chief. Runs the Neutral information network and the only honest broker around.

## Personality

- Fast-talking
- Mischievous
- Secretly kind
- Allergic to sincerity until it counts

## Visual Concept

Courier's jacket covered in trade pins, a hand-cranked radio, perpetually mid-snack.

## Relationship to the Player

Best-friend energy and comic Greek chorus; feeds the player intel and the occasional hard truth.

## Secrets / Emotional Wounds

- Knows who's rigging the rations and is sitting on it
- Lost someone in the early days and deflects with jokes
- Is the only one who knows the player's full name

## Possible Route Arc

- Comic relief → information broker → forced to take a real side at the Vote

## Sample Dialogue

> Greenhaven, population: dwindling, drama: thriving.

> Rule one: never trust a man with a clipboard. Rule two: that includes Sable. Especially Sable.

> (dropping the bit) I joke because the alternative is counting who's missing. Anyway! Casserole?
